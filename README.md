# Price to Sales Ratio: Meaning, Formula and Importance in Stock Market

The **Price to Sales Ratio** is an important financial metric used by investors to evaluate a company's stock valuation based on its revenue. It helps investors understand how much the market is willing to pay for every rupee of sales generated by a business.

Investors can explore more fundamental analysis concepts through [Finowings](https://www.finowings.com/).

## What Is the Price to Sales Ratio?

The Price to Sales (P/S) Ratio compares a company's market capitalisation with its total annual revenue. It is particularly useful when analysing companies that generate revenue but have low profits or are not yet profitable.

## Price to Sales Ratio Formula

The formula is:

```text
Price to Sales Ratio = Market Capitalisation / Annual Revenue
```

### Example

Suppose a company has:

- Market Capitalisation: ₹5,000 crore
- Annual Revenue: ₹2,500 crore

**Calculation:**

```text
P/S Ratio = 5,000 / 2,500
P/S Ratio = 2
```

This means investors are paying ₹2 for every ₹1 of revenue generated by the company.

## Why Is the P/S Ratio Important?

The P/S ratio helps investors:

- Compare the valuations of companies in the same industry.
- Understand how the market values business revenue.
- Evaluate companies that are not yet profitable.
- Identify potentially expensive or attractively valued stocks.

## Limitations of the P/S Ratio

A lower P/S ratio does not always mean a stock is undervalued. This metric does not consider profitability, debt, operating expenses or cash flow.

Therefore, investors should compare it with other financial indicators, such as:

- Price-to-Earnings (P/E) Ratio
- Return on Equity (ROE)
- Debt-to-Equity Ratio

## Conclusion

The Price to Sales Ratio is a useful tool for fundamental analysis and stock valuation. However, investors should consider revenue growth, profitability and industry conditions before making investment decisions.
